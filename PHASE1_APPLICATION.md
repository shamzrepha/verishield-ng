## Step 3 of 5 — Your Idea

- **Do you already have a solution idea? `*` (`has_solution_idea`):**
  > Select **`Yes, I have an idea`**

- **If Yes, what problem are you trying to solve? `*` (`problem_to_solve`):**
  > `VeriShield NG addresses two high-impact synthetic-media and integrity threats facing Nigeria's 2026–2027 elections: (1) Citizen-facing blackout-window disinformation—specifically AI voice-cloned WhatsApp voice notes and doctored "INEC Press Release" flyers claiming polls are postponed, relocated, or cancelled, which disproportionately deceive multilingual speakers and blind/low-vision voters because existing text fact-checkers do not inspect voice notes or manipulated screenshots; and (2) Registration/intake visual spoofing and insider upload anomalies—where synthetic AI-generated faces, recaptured phone-screen photos, or abnormal off-hours batch uploads threaten intake data quality. Both problems lack a unified, nonpartisan, privacy-preserving forensic screening tool built for Nigerian languages and real-world WhatsApp compression.`

- **Who is affected by this problem? `*` (`affected_people`):**
  > `Everyday Nigerian voters—especially first-time voters, rural and peri-urban WhatsApp users, speakers of Igbo, Pidgin, Yoruba, and Hausa, and blind or low-vision voters who rely on audio rather than English text—are directly vulnerable to synthetic audio and forged-flyer suppression tactics. Civic fact-checkers, accredited election observers, and electoral integrity teams are also affected by high-volume multimodal rumours and manual visual/metadata triage across intake workflows.`

- **Describe your proposed solution `*` (`proposed_solution`):**
  > `VeriShield NG is a unified, nonpartisan, senior-friendly AI defense platform built on one Shared Synthetic-Media & Tamper Forensics Core with three simple, accessible pillars:
  > 1. **Conversational Civic AI PWA & Media Shield (Module A):** Designed so everyday voters—including elderly or blind/low-vision citizens—can chat or speak in plain English or Pidgin (e.g., `"them say peter obi don drop from election"`), cross-check rumors against INEC timetables, Electoral Act 2022 provisions, and live public Nigerian fact-checks (Dubawa & FactCheckHub), or attach suspicious WhatsApp voice notes and flyers. Our Shared Forensics Core screens audio/visual tampering, calibrates uncertainty (`"Inconclusive"` under WhatsApp compression), and reads replies aloud in Igbo, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, or English.
  > 2. **Consent-Gated GPS Polling Unit Matcher & Shake-to-Alert Multi-Witness Shield:** With explicit NDPA 2023 opt-in consent, citizens can match device GPS ephemerally to their nearest Polling Unit (coordinates immediately discarded) and arm accelerometer Shake-to-Alert panic detection inside the installable Mobile PWA. When multiple users near a Polling Unit trigger alerts, a sandboxed warning alerts nearby citizens/observers; once safe, a conversational AI interviewer confirms direct eyewitness vs. secondhand certainty across multiple witnesses to produce a balanced summary without half-stories or partisan spin.
  > 3. **INEC Intake Screening Desk (Module B — `/screening`):** Runs the exact same Visual Forensics Core on strictly synthetic intake records (zero real voter PII) to flag AI-generated portraits, screen-recapture spoofs, duplicate faces, and off-hours upload bursts into a human-in-the-loop Supervisor Review Queue.`

- **How would AI or technology contribute to the solution? `*` (`technology_contribution`):**
  > `Rather than acting as a generic LLM wrapper, VeriShield NG combines targeted forensic, live fact-checking, and corroboration components:
  > - Pidgin-Aware Conversational Intent & Live Fact-Check Lookup: Maps natural-language rumors (including Nigerian Pidgin "them say..." withdrawal rumours) to INEC timetables, Electoral Act 2022 provisions, public Dubawa/FactCheckHub JSON feeds, and optional pluggable LLM synthesis (with graceful offline/quota fallback) while blocking partisan advice or result declarations.
  > - Acoustic & Shared Visual Forensics Core: Spectral/silence-floor anti-spoofing with WhatsApp Opus narrowband calibration, plus blockwise bidirectional ELA splice localization, 2D-FFT moiré/glare recapture detection, smooth-patch micro-noise analysis, and perceptual dHash duplicate clustering.
  > - Privacy-Preserving PWA Telemetry & Multi-Witness Corroboration: Ephemeral Haversine Polling Unit lookup, opt-in DeviceMotion shake clustering, and conversational AI witness interviewing weighting direct eyewitness accounts vs. secondhand reports.
  > - Accessibility-First Design: Multi-page Mobile PWA (`/`, `/citizen`, `/screening`) with Bigger Words and High-Contrast modes, WCAG focus/ARIA support, voice input, and localized spoken replies in Igbo, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, and English.`

- **Who would benefit from the solution? `*` (`beneficiaries`):**
  > `1. Nigerian voters—particularly blind/low-vision citizens and Igbo, Pidgin, Yoruba, and Hausa speakers—who gain fast, accessible, spoken verification of viral election audio and flyers.
  > 2. Civil society organizations, accredited observer networks, and election fact-checkers who can triage multimodal WhatsApp rumours faster with explainable forensic evidence.
  > 3. Electoral technology and data-quality teams who gain a privacy-safe blueprint and human-in-the-loop audit workflow for flagging synthetic portraits and anomalous batch uploads without automated disenfranchisement.`

- **What makes your approach different or useful? `*` (`differentiation`):**
  > `Four design choices distinguish VeriShield NG:
  > 1. Shared Forensics Core across two threats: One audited visual forensics engine powers both citizen flyer verification and synthetic intake spoof detection, reducing maintenance overhead and improving reliability.
  > 2. Compression-aware calibrated uncertainty: We explicitly benchmark against WhatsApp .ogg/.opus degradation and refuse overconfident claims, defaulting to "Inconclusive — Verify via Official INEC Channels" when signal fidelity drops.
  > 3. Accessibility & Nigerian languages as first-class architecture: Spoken 20-second verdicts in Igbo, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, and English plus screen-reader-native UI reach voters left behind by English text fact-checks.
  > 4. Strict Responsible-AI & zero-PII guardrails: Strictly nonpartisan, never tabulates or declares election results, never auto-rejects any record without human review, and operates exclusively on synthetic and public data.`
