AI Transparency & NDPA 2023 Data Minimization Disclosure

How AI is used: VeriShield NG uses automated forensics, Nigerian Pidgin/civic intent matching, live public fact-check search (Dubawa & FactCheckHub), and verified INEC public documents. We never predict election winners or declare results.
Your privacy: Zero real voter PII is stored. Inside the Citizen App, GPS location matching and Shake-sensor monitoring are turned off by default and require your explicit checkbox consent first. When GPS is used to find your nearest polling unit, coordinates are discarded immediately after matching.
